Engineering Rationale for 15,000 Supervoxels

Moving from 5k to 15k yields substantial Dice gains (+2.06% WT, +1.88% TC, +1.83% ET). Going to 20k exhibits diminishing returns while increasing graph node count to ~40,000, confirming 15,000 as the optimal Pareto sweet-spot.
